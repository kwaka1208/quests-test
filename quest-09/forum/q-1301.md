# TypeError: unsupported operand type(s) for +: 'int' and 'str'

**Asked by** @tomoki-s · **Tags:** `python` · **Score:** 15

I wrote `total = 10 + input("How many? ")` and I get this error. Why?

---

## Answer · Score: 33 · ✔ Accepted · answered 2019

`input()` always returns a string. Change it to a number first:

```python
total = 10 + int(input("How many? "))
```
