# How do I print today's menu with kitchen?

**Asked by** @yui-dev · **Tags:** `python` `kitchen` · **Score:** 12

---

## Answer · Score: 20 · ✔ Accepted · answered 2024

```python
menu = kitchen.get_menu()
for name, price in menu.items():
    print(name, price)
```

- **@haruka-m** (2026): This is for kitchen 2.x. It does not work with kitchen 3.0.

## Answer · Score: 35 · answered 2026

For kitchen 3.0 and later:

```python
for name, price in kitchen.fetch_menu():
    print(name, price)
```

`fetch_menu()` returns a list of pairs, so you do not need `.items()`.
