This file keeps old notes about the office Wi-Fi.

## History of the Wi-Fi

The office was small, and the Wi-Fi was open.
Anyone could join it without a word.

## Wi-Fi Words (Version 1)

In version 1, the secret word was **anchor**.
This word was removed in version 2 and does not work anymore.

## Deprecated: Old Wi-Fi Words

The words below are deprecated. Please do not use them:

- `sailor`
- `anchor`

They were replaced in version 3.
